GANDOPS is a client-side operations research demonstration. It does not connect to production environments or automatically detect actual incidents.

  1. MeasureSupply availability, errors, and P95 latency from a synthetic or authorized source.
  2. ScoreApply visible, illustrative weights to identify rising operational risk.
